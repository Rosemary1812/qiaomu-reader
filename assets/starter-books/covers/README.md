# Starter cover artwork

These six images are published book covers or historic cover scans, not generated illustrations. They are distributed separately from the plugin GPL license. Source pages were checked on 2026-09-09; the catalog records their rights statements, source and image URLs, processing, byte sizes and SHA-256 digests. No endorsement by the source institutions is implied.

The artwork is used to identify the work. The text remains the pinned Project Gutenberg edition, which may differ in translation, typography or publication date from the pictured cover. Each EPUB includes `EPUB/cover-credits.txt`; its reading spine and original source/license chapter remain unchanged.

## Maintenance

The three English images are Standard Ebooks' published small cover JPEGs, released under CC0. Chinese images retain the historic lettering, paper and cloth texture; no watermark is removed. The Chinese images are resized to 320 pixels wide and encoded with Pillow as optimized progressive RGB JPEG at quality 68. The source metadata and rights statements, rather than the age of the literary work alone, must be checked when replacing artwork.

Normal builds are offline. To replace an image, download from the recorded source, inspect it, update its catalog hash, then run `npm run build:books`. Do not replace a user's imported EPUB during artwork updates.
